# This is a J1 Summary Project
- It is a simple MUD game about “Tung Town”: a small, tangled village which once used to be peaceful, but was taken over by “brainrot” monsters such as Tralalero Tralala, Bombardino Crocadillo and Ballerina Cappucina. The tone is playful and slightly eerie rather than grim.
- Mechanically, it is a classic hunt-the-monsters maze: the Player explores different landmarks of the village and confronts multiple monsters along the way. Defeated monsters drop special weapons or items that the Player can use to fight other monsters. Eventually, the player confronts Devil Tung— the evil companion who sabotages the Player the whole game. The Angel Tung serves as a helper, healing the Player during fights and providing tips.
